# Guide: Taking Your Website Online

To make your "Akash House Keeping & Cleaning Services" website accessible to anyone on the internet, you need two main things: a Domain Name (your website's address) and Web Hosting (storage for your website's files). 

Here are the step-by-step instructions to take your localhost site online:

## Step 1: Buy a Domain Name (The Web Address)
A domain name is your online identity (e.g., `akashcleaning.com` or `akashhousekeeping.in`).
1. Go to a domain registrar like **GoDaddy**, **Namecheap**, **Hostinger**, or **BigRock**.
2. Search for your desired name to see if it's available.
3. Once you find an available name, add it to your cart and complete the purchase (usually costs around $10-$15 per year or ₹500-₹1000/year).

## Step 2: Choose Web Hosting (Storage for Files)
Web hosting is where your HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and image files will live. Since your website is a static site (HTML/CSS/JS without a backend database like PHP/MySQL), you have some excellent free options as well as paid ones.

**Option A: Free Static Hosting (Highly Recommended for this site)**
Services like **Netlify**, **Vercel**, or **GitHub Pages** offer excellent, fast, and free hosting for static sites.
- **Netlify/Vercel:** You can simply drag and drop your entire project folder onto their website, and it will be live instantly.

**Option B: Paid Shared Hosting**
If you want professional email accounts (like `contact@akashcleaning.com`) along with your hosting, you might want shared hosting from **Hostinger**, **Bluehost**, or **GoDaddy**.

## Step 3: Upload Your Files
*If using Netlify (Easiest Method):*
1. Create a free account on [Netlify](https://www.netlify.com/).
2. Go to the "Sites" tab.
3. Drag and drop your entire `house cleaning` folder (containing your `index.html`, `style.css`, etc.) into the designated upload box.
4. Netlify will instantly generate a live link for your website.

*If using Paid Shared Hosting:*
1. Log into your hosting account's Control Panel (cPanel).
2. Open the **File Manager**.
3. Navigate to the `public_html` folder.
4. Upload all the files and folders from your `house cleaning` project (make sure `index.html` is in the main `public_html` directory).

## Step 4: Connect Your Domain to Your Hosting
If you bought your domain and hosting from the same company, this is usually done automatically. If you used Netlify or a separate hosting provider:
1. Go to your domain registrar (where you bought the domain).
2. Find the **DNS Settings** or **Manage DNS**.
3. You will need to change the "Nameservers" or add an "A Record" and "CNAME" pointing to your hosting provider. (Your hosting provider will give you the exact values to enter).
4. For Netlify: Go to Domain Management in your Netlify dashboard, click "Add custom domain", type in your purchased domain, and follow their step-by-step instructions to update your DNS records.

## Summary Checklist
- [ ] Buy a Domain Name
- [ ] Choose a Hosting Provider (Netlify is recommended for a free and easy start)
- [ ] Upload your files (`index.html`, `style.css`, `app.js`, `assets/`, etc.)
- [ ] Point your Domain to your Hosting provider via DNS Settings
- [ ] Wait for DNS propagation (can take anywhere from 1 to 24 hours)
- [ ] Your site is live!
